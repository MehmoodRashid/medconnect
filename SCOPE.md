# MedConnect FYP-I Scope

## In scope
1. Patient registration and profile (health history)
2. Adaptive symptom and lifestyle questionnaire
3. Rule-based symptom-to-specialty recommendation
4. Doctor registration and login
5. Doctor view of patient history
6. Doctor directory, availability, appointment requests and status
7. Core database design (extensible)
8. Responsive web UI: patient interface and doctor dashboard
9. Design documents for extended modules (no implementation)

## Design-only (docs/, no code)
Medicine Shop, Price Comparison, Patient Support Team, Video Consultation

## Excluded (FYP-II)
AI/ML diagnosis, test recommendation models, payments, WebRTC, Socket.IO, pharmacy integrations
